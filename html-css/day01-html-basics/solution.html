Hiking

I enjoy hiking because it gets me outdoors and away from screens for a few hours. It's also a great, low-cost way to explore new places near where I live.

Things I Need to Get Started

  • A sturdy pair of hiking boots
  • A water bottle
  • A small backpack

Steps To Get Started

  1. Buy proper footwear
  2. Find a beginner-friendly trail nearby
  3. Go with a friend the first few times
