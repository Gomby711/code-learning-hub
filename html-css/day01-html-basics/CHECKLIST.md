# Day 1 Checklist

Open your edited `starter.html` in a browser and confirm:

- [ ] Exactly one `<h1>` is visible, larger/bolder than everything else on the page
- [ ] A paragraph of at least two sentences appears below it
- [ ] Two `<h2>` headings appear, visually smaller than the `<h1>` but larger than the paragraph text
- [ ] A bulleted list (unordered) appears under the first `<h2>`, with at least 3 items
- [ ] A numbered list (ordered) appears under the second `<h2>`, with at least 3 items, automatically numbered 1, 2, 3 by the browser (you should NOT have typed the numbers yourself)
- [ ] An image is visible on the page
- [ ] Right-click the image → Inspect → confirm in DevTools that your `alt` attribute is present and actually describes the image (not empty, not just the filename)
- [ ] A link is visible with descriptive text (not "click here") and clicking it opens the correct destination
- [ ] A "Back to top" link at the bottom of the page, when clicked, scrolls the page back up to your `<h1>`
- [ ] View the page source (Ctrl+U, or DevTools → Elements) and confirm your `<h1>` has `id="top"` and your bottom link has `href="#top"`

Compare against `solution.html` if anything is unclear.
