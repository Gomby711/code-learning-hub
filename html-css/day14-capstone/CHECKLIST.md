# Day 14 Checklist

- [ ] At least 3 pages exist and link to each other correctly using relative paths
- [ ] The navbar and footer are identical (or near-identical, e.g. highlighting the current page) across all pages
- [ ] The homepage shows a hero section and a Grid-based preview of featured content
- [ ] The contact page's form fields show `:valid`/`:invalid` styling only after interaction, not on page load
- [ ] At 375px width (DevTools device toolbar), the layout is usable, readable, and doesn't overflow horizontally
- [ ] At 1200px+ width, the layout takes advantage of the extra space (not just a narrow mobile layout stretched wide)
- [ ] At least one meaningful layout change (not just font-size) happens at your media query breakpoint
- [ ] Class names for your navbar, cards, and buttons follow BEM (`block__element--modifier`)
- [ ] At least one `:hover`/`:focus` transition is present and feels smooth, not an instant snap
- [ ] `:root` custom properties define your primary color(s), used via `var()` throughout
- [ ] Every image has meaningful `alt` text; every form input has a `<label for>` matching its `id`
- [ ] `box-sizing: border-box` and a margin/padding reset are the first rules in your stylesheet
- [ ] You did NOT open `solution/` until you'd made a genuine attempt of your own

Compare against `solution/` once you've made a genuine attempt.
