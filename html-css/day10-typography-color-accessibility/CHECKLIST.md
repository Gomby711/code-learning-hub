# Day 10 Checklist

- [ ] Body text uses a sans-serif font and is visibly comfortable to read, with clear space between lines
- [ ] In DevTools, checking the "low-contrast-button" shows a contrast ratio that FAILS WCAG AA (a red/warning indicator)
- [ ] In DevTools, checking the "good-contrast-button" shows a contrast ratio that PASSES WCAG AA (at least 4.5:1)
- [ ] The image has a meaningful `alt` attribute (check via View Source or DevTools) describing what it shows, not empty and not the filename
- [ ] You can explain, in your own words, the difference between hex, rgb, and hsl color formats, and why hsl is often easier to reason about when creating a family of related shades
- [ ] You can list, from memory, at least 4 of the 5 "pulling it all together" accessibility practices from today's lesson

Compare against `solution.css` if anything is unclear.
